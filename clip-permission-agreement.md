# Clip Permission Agreement (Template)

*Starting template, not legal advice. Replace every [bracket]. Delete options you don't use.*

**Date:** [DATE]

**Creator:** [Creator name], who streams as [channel name] on [platform(s)].
**Editor:** [Editor name], contact [editor email].

## 1. Work
The Editor will make short edited clips ("Clips") from the Creator's recorded streams and videos ("Source Content"). Agreed amount and price:

- [ ] Trial: [__] clips from [__] recording(s), [free / $__]
- [ ] Monthly: [__] clips per month at [$__] per month
- [ ] Per clip: [__] clips at [$__] each

## 2. Permission to use Source Content
2.1 The Creator gives the Editor a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable permission, which the Creator may withdraw at any time, to access, copy and edit the Source Content **only to make Clips for the Creator**.
2.2 Covered Source Content: [ ] recordings the Creator sends / [ ] public recordings at [channel URL].
2.3 The Editor will not post, sell, license or monetize Source Content or Clips, and will not share them with anyone except the Creator, unless section 5 applies.
2.4 The Editor will delete all Source Content and working files within [30] days after this agreement ends, or sooner if the Creator asks in writing, and will confirm in writing.

## 3. Creator's responsibilities
3.1 The Creator confirms they have the right to give this permission, including any consent needed from guests, co-streamers or others who appear.
3.2 The Creator lists anything that must not be clipped in the do-not-clip list and updates it when needed.
3.3 The Creator decides whether and where each Clip is published.

## 4. Ownership and money
4.1 The Creator keeps all rights in the Source Content.
4.2 After payment for the relevant period, the Editor assigns to the Creator whatever rights the Editor has in the Clips, including edits, captions and titles written for them.
4.3 The Creator keeps 100% of any revenue the Clips earn.

## 5. Optional permissions (default is NO; tick to allow)
- [ ] **Scheduling access:** the Editor may schedule Clips the Creator has approved on: [accounts]. Revocable at any time.
- [ ] **Portfolio use:** the Editor may show up to [3] approved Clips, credited to the Creator, to prospective clients. The Creator can withdraw this in writing and the Editor removes them within [7] days.

## 6. Review
The Editor delivers Clips to [shared folder or channel]. The Creator gets [1/2] revision round(s) per Clip. A Clip is approved when the Creator says so or posts it.

## 7. Term and payment
7.1 Payment: [method], [in advance monthly / on delivery]. Late payment pauses delivery.
7.2 Either side may end this agreement with [7] days' written notice (email counts). Already-paid Clips are still delivered.
7.3 If the Creator withdraws permission under section 2, the agreement ends immediately.

## 8. Tools and rules
8.1 The Editor may use editing, captioning or detection software. Every Clip is checked against the do-not-clip list before delivery. If a platform requires labels for altered or synthetic content, the Editor flags it and the Creator applies the label when posting.
8.2 The Editor will not add third-party music, footage or images without the Creator's written approval and a valid license.
8.3 Except for willful misconduct, each side's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the [3] months before the claim.

## 9. Confidentiality
The Editor keeps private any non-public information the Creator shares (announcements, sponsor terms, account access, private messages visible in footage).

## 10. General
Governing law: [state or country]. This is the whole agreement on this subject; changes must be in writing and agreed by both sides (a confirming email is fine).
